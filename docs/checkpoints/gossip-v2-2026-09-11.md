## Deliberate boundaries

- Gossip exchanges, verifies, enriches, and propagates market intelligence.
- Gossip does not prepare, sign, submit, or execute trades or transactions.
- `private_submission`, `gossip_feedback`, payment, generic signing, and trading
  remain inaccessible.
- Frozen `gossip.evidence.v1` requires at least one source, and every source in
  a public submission must be public.
