## Product boundary

Gossip exchanges, verifies, enriches, and propagates market intelligence.
It does not prepare, sign, submit, or execute trades or transactions.

`private_submission`, `gossip_feedback`, payments, generic signing, and trading
remain inaccessible. Public submissions require public evidence and zero
protocol cost.
